# GAIL Gas PNG Connection Registration – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

## **1. General & Contact Information**

### **Q: What city or region should I select when filling out the form?**

Select your city/GA (e.g., Bengaluru GA) from the dropdown list at both the top of the form and in the address section.

### **Q: What customer type should I choose?**

Select **Postpaid Connection** under the "Select Customer Type" dropdown.

### **Q: Whose name should be entered under the main applicant field?**

Enter the full name of the primary applicant (including surname) exactly as it appears on official government identity documents.

### **Q: Which contact details are mandatory?**

* **Mandatory:** Full Name, Father’s/Mother’s/Husband’s Name, Primary Mobile Number, and Primary Email Address.


* **Optional:** Additional Email, Alternate Mobile Number, and Landline Number.



---

## **2. Address & Property Details**

### **Q: How do I enter my address accurately?**

Fill out all mandatory address fields marked with a red asterisk:

* **Type Of House:** Select house structure type.


* **Floor & House/Flat No:** Specify floor level and exact flat/unit number.


* **Society/Apartment:** Enter full apartment complex or society name.


* **Street/Area Name & Area:** Enter local street name and select local area.


* **Pin & Landmark:** Provide 6-digit PIN code and a recognizable nearby landmark.



### **Q: What option should I select under "Type Of Ownership"?**

Select **Owner** if you own the property, or **Rented** if you are currently renting.

---

## **3. Payment Plans & Existing Gas Connections**

### **Q: What payment plan options are available for new connections?**

* **Standard Deposit Option:** ₹4,000 refundable connection deposit + ₹500 refundable gas consumption deposit.
* **Instalment Option (EDI):** ₹5,000 collected at ₹5/day over 1,000 days + ₹500 consumption deposit.
* **FLEXI Rental Scheme:** Non-refundable rental fee of ₹100 + GST bi-monthly in place of upfront connection deposit + ₹500 consumption deposit.

### **Q: What should I select under "Do You Have LPG Connection?"**

Select **YES** if you currently hold an active LPG cylinder connection (e.g., Indane, HP, Bharat Gas), or **NO** if you do not.

---

## **4. Required Documents & File Specs**

### **Q: What documents must I upload?**

1. **Proof of Identity (Mandatory):** Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License.


2. **Proof of Ownership / Tenancy (Mandatory):** Sale Deed, Latest Electricity Bill, Tax Receipt, Rent Agreement, or Society Certificate.


3. **No Objection Certificate (NOC):** Required for rented premises or specific multi-story layouts.


4. **Others (Optional):** Any additional supporting documents.



### **Q: What are the file format and size limits for document uploads?**

* **Supporting Documents (Identity/Ownership/NOC):** Formats: **JPEG, JPG, PDF, PNG** | Maximum Size: **2 MB** per file.


* **Applicant Signature:** Formats: **JPEG, JPG, PNG** | Maximum Size: **200 KB**.



---

## **5. Submission & Verification**

### **Q: How do I successfully submit the online form?**

Type the exact characters shown in the CAPTCHA box into **Input symbols** and click **Save**. Click **Clear** if you need to reset all form fields.
